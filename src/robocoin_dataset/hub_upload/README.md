# 🤖 Consent Required for RoboCOIN Dataset Access

Thank you for your interest in **RoboCOIN** — an open-source dataset for **bimanual robotic manipulation**! To help us sustain and improve this resource for the global robotics community, we kindly ask you to complete a brief, one-time consent form before downloading.

---

### Why We Ask

As developers of RoboCOIN, we’re committed to making high-quality bimanual robot data accessible to researchers everywhere. To do that effectively, we’d like to understand:

- Your **country/region** (e.g., "South Korea", "Brazil", "Germany")
- Your **affiliation** (e.g., "ETH Zurich", "Boston Dynamics", "Independent Researcher")
- Your **intended use** (e.g., "imitation learning", "policy generalization", "teaching demo")

This information enables us to:
✅ Prioritize support for underrepresented regions (e.g., localized documentation or calibration tools)
✅ Tailor examples and tutorials to real-world research workflows
✅ Demonstrate global impact when applying for grants or institutional support
✅ Build stronger connections with academic and industrial robotics communities

---

### Your Privacy, Our Commitment

We take your trust seriously. Here’s what we **do NOT do**:
- ❌ Collect emails, IP addresses, or personal identifiers
- ❌ Share, sell, or monetize your responses
- ❌ Use your data for marketing or tracking

All submissions are stored **anonymously** and used only in aggregate (e.g., “15% of users from Africa”). This is purely to guide the future development of **RoboCOIN** as a public good.

> *Open robotics advances through collaboration. By sharing a few details, you help ensure RoboCOIN remains free, relevant, and inclusive for everyone.*

---

### 🚀 How to Proceed

1. **Request access** to this consent dataset:
   → [Fill out the quick form here](https://huggingface.co/datasets/RoboCOIN/gate)

You’ll only need to do this **once**, ever.
